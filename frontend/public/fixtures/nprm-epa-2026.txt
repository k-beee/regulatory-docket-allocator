Control of Air Pollution From New Motor Vehicles: Heavy-Duty Engine and Vehicle Standards

AGENCY: Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).
ACTION: Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM) and Public Hearing.

SUMMARY: The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) is proposing revised particulate matter (PM2.5) and nitrogen oxide (NOx) emissions standards under Section 202(a) of the Clean Air Act for heavy-duty commercial motor vehicles beginning with model year 2028. This administrative docket incorporates an algorithmic sortition hearing procedure under 5 U.S.C. § 553 to ensure non-arbitrary, balanced representation of diverse stakeholder perspectives during public oral deliberations.
