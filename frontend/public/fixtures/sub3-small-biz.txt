SUBJECT: Rural Depot Electrification, Grid Interconnection Delays, and Capital Barriers

Independent owner-operators represent 38% of long-haul freight capacity. Small fleet operators face 18 to 36 month transformer lead times from regional electric utilities when attempting to construct depot-level megawatt charging facilities. Without dedicated Small Business Administration (SBA) loan guarantees and rural grid exceptions, the proposed rulemaking will force small operators out of the market.
