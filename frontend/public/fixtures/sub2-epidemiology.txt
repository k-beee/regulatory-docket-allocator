SUBJECT: Longitudinal Epidemiology on PM2.5 Exposure Along Interstate Freight Corridors

We submit peer-reviewed epidemiological data from 14 metropolitan transport hubs showing an immediate 18.4% reduction in pediatric asthma hospitalizations corresponding to accelerated heavy diesel turnover. Under 5 U.S.C. § 553(c), the EPA must prioritize immediate public health criteria over speculative commercial amortization models, as particulate exposure causes irreversible pulmonary damage.
